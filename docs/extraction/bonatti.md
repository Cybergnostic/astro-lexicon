# Guido Bonatti — extraction ledger

Source: *Liber Astronomiae*, Project Hindsight / Cazimi Press English translations in the initial corpus.

- PDF pp. 70–84: Second Tractate, Part I, Chapters I–VII, reviewed completely.
- PDF pp. 85–152: Second Tractate, Part II, Chapters I–XXVIII, reviewed completely.

- PDF pp. 300–343: Third Tractatus, Part II, reviewed completely. The Third Tractatus ends at PDF p. 343.

- PDF pp. 360–368: Treatise 4, *Conjunctions*, Chapters 1–8, reviewed completely.

## Editorial rule applied

Only evidence-grade A or B material is stored as a source interpretation. Polemical repetition, historical name lists, incidental examples, long correspondence catalogues and mere mentions are skipped. Translator/editor commentary is not attributed to Bonatti.

Bonatti’s terminology around “astrology” and “astronomy” is source-specific and not normalized silently to modern usage. Likewise, overlapping medieval vocabulary such as fall/descension/detriment is preserved in interpretation notes rather than silently harmonized.

The Second Tractate, Part III pass preserves Bonatti’s own definition of Aym / “own similitude” independently from Robert Hand’s editorial identification with hayz.

The Third Tractatus adds especially important operational definitions for Almuguea, Alitifal/application, Dustoria/Hayz, planetary orbs, latitude application, void course versus feral/rustic condition, transfer of nature, return and abscission of light, prohibition, reception as donation of virtue, return of virtue, refranation, contrariety, frustration, besiegement, and the opening of the gates. These are kept distinct where Bonatti distinguishes their mechanisms.

Treatise 4 adds the conjunction hierarchy used in mundane astrology, the special Saturn–Mars conjunction in Cancer, the distinct combust/incombust hour cycle after a lunation, the Moon’s temporal duodena, and Bonatti’s syzygy-based Ascendant rectification procedure. The combust-hour doctrine is kept separate from planetary combustion, and the Moon’s duodena is kept separate from zodiacal twelfth-parts.
