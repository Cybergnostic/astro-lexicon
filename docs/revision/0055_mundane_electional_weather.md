# Revision batch 9 — mundane, electional and weather terminology

Implemented in `db/seed/0055_cross_source_revision_mundane_electional_weather.sql`.

## Strict lexicality pruning

Several extraction labels were useful while reading but do not deserve independent lexicon entries:

- **Saturn-Mars Conjunction in Cancer** — a particular configuration and application of conjunction/dignity/reception doctrine, not a named reusable concept.
- **Eclipse Duration Rule** — an editorial label for a transmitted timing rule, not a stable term.
- **Comet Judgment** — an extraction heading for a method and catalogue rather than a source-defined lexical item.
- **Electional Timing** — a collection of timing rules inside electional doctrine, not an independently delimited term in Bonatti.

The underlying passages remain searchable source evidence; only the manufactured ontology entries are removed.

## Animodar duplicate removed

Bonatti’s provisional **Syzygy Almuten Rectification** entry is merged into **Animodar / Namudar**. The procedure has the same defining structure: preceding conjunction/opposition, planet prevailing by dignity there, and correction of the Ascendant from that planet.

**Conjunctional Nativity** and **Preventional Nativity** remain separate quality-5 branch terms because the preceding conjunction-versus-opposition distinction is repeatedly operative in rectification, lots and longevity doctrine.

## Elections

Publication-ready definitions now cover:

- **Election** — deliberate selection of a fitting inception, not the cause of the act itself;
- **Root of an Election** — preferably a nativity or radical question against which the election is tested;
- **Electional Adaptation** — strengthening and appropriately arranging the principal electional factors;
- **Inception** — the first intrinsic act of the undertaking rather than preliminary preparation.

The more generic extraction label Electional Timing was removed rather than turning a bundle of rules into a noun-term.

## Mundane annual vocabulary

Publication-ready or strong entries now include:

- Revolution of the Year of the World;
- Luminary of the Time;
- Significator of the King;
- Clime;
- Great Conjunction Cycle.

The Great Conjunction Cycle remains quality 4 because Bonatti’s hierarchy is clear but its exact period numbers belong to his source interpretation rather than to a supposedly universal definition.

## Fixed-star vocabulary

- **Fixed-Star Conjunction** — quality 5; exact or near-exact zodiacal conjunction, stronger as exactness increases.
- **Benefic Fixed Star** and **Malefic Fixed Star** — quality 4 contextual historical classifications rather than absolute astronomical properties.

## Weather terminology

al-Bīrūnī’s **Opening the Doors** is now quality 5 and remains explicitly distinct from Bonatti’s reception-based **Opening of the Gates**.

Bonatti’s meteorological categories are retained where they are genuinely defined:

- Convertible Planet;
- Wet Lunar Mansion;
- Dry Lunar Mansion;
- Common / Temperate Lunar Mansion;
- Lunar Dichotomy.

## Post-lunation hour doctrines

**Combust Hours** and **Incombust Hours** are quality 5 because Bonatti repeatedly preserves the stable twelve-hour / seventy-two-hour cycle.

**Duodena of the Moon / Lunar Hour Cycle** remains quality 4. Treatise 4 and Treatise 9 transmit related but non-identical organizations of the hour sequence, so both source accounts are preserved without inventing a harmonized schedule.
