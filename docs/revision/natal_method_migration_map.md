# Natal-method migration map

Status date: 2026-08-09

Purpose: classify the high-dependency natal-method lessons by what can be migrated into the public lexicon now, what needs another historical witness, and what should remain private/course-specific unless independently corroborated.

## 1. Natal synthesis / complete-chart workflow

**Status: editorial workflow, not a standalone doctrine article.**

The meta-framework is valuable as a sequencing and coverage map: establish chart validity and basic condition, judge foundational natal structure, then move into specialized topics and timing. It should guide article dependencies and procedural navigation rather than become a public page that reproduces the private course syllabus.

Public migration target: relations between existing canonical terms and, later, a general natal-judgment/procedure page if the historical corpus supplies a stable source basis.

## 2. Primary Motivation

**Status: private/course-method only pending independent historical corroboration.**

The legacy distillation is structurally good and preserves its source evidence. Its four-element motivation taxonomy and the exact Ascendant-based procedure are nevertheless specific enough that they should not be presented as universal traditional doctrine merely because they occur in the course.

Current action:

- retain the distilled records as an internal pedagogy/coverage map;
- do not create a reviewed public `primary-motivation` doctrine entry yet;
- search later historical additions for an independently attested equivalent method;
- if corroborated, map only the historically shared portion and keep course-specific sequencing private.

## 3. Almuten of the Figure

**Status: public/canonical; migration active.**

The canonical database already contains `almuten` and `almuten-of-figure`, with the distinction between topic-specific predominance and whole-chart predominance preserved. The private curriculum adds pedagogical emphasis but is not needed as public authority.

Current action:

- expanded the public article for whole-chart predominance;
- explicitly separated the Almuten of the Figure from a topic-specific almuten;
- kept scoring tables and weighting variants source-specific rather than importing one private procedure as universal.

## 4. Ruler of Behavior / Ruler of Manners

**Status: historically plausible public term, but requires public-source onboarding before publication.**

The distilled lesson contains a coherent selection procedure and attributed historical material on planetary rulers of manners. This is not being discarded. However, the relevant historical witness used in the lesson is outside the current five-work public corpus.

Current action:

- preserve the legacy distillation intact as the migration source;
- do not publish its selection hierarchy from private course evidence alone;
- add an appropriate historical manners source to the future source-onboarding queue;
- once independently extracted, create or normalize the canonical public term and attach the reviewed procedure to that historical evidence.

Worked examples remain examples only and are not evidence for the universal rule.

## 5. Quality of Mind

**Status: mixed migration. General structure is portable; exact course recipe remains private until independently sourced.**

The lesson distinguishes a Mercury-centered rational/intellectual layer from a Moon-centered perceptive/instinctive layer and then modifies both through planetary condition, rulers and close contacts. Several component doctrines already belong naturally to existing public terms.

Current public migration:

- Mercury article: rational thought, speech, calculation and the need to judge condition rather than sign alone;
- Moon article: perception, habit, receptivity and changing response;
- existing terms for speed, direct/retrograde motion, station, solar visibility, dignity, house strength and aspects provide the technical dependency graph.

Not promoted yet:

- the exact ordered five-factor recipe from the private lesson;
- course-specific psychological adjectives attached to every condition;
- claims that need a separate historical mind/manners witness.

The natal-method block does **not** require fresh raw extraction. The existing distillations are doing their intended job: they reveal which public terms need richer explanation and which methods are too course-specific to publish without another historical witness.
