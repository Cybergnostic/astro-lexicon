## Historical safeguards

- al-Biruni, Book of Instruction §475 describes a Part of Fortune construction and reports authors who reverse the nocturnal ordering. Do not impose a single formula across authors.
- Lots with similar English names may have technically distinct source formulas. Do not merge Eros, Necessity, or gendered marriage/children lots on vocabulary alone.
- Preserve normalized tables, alias relationships, source interpretations and historical attribution.
- A machine flag never authorizes a factual edit. Use exact work, edition and passage references.
