# al-Bīrūnī — extraction ledger

Source: *The Book of Instruction in the Elements of the Art of Astrology* (R. Ramsay Wright translation, 1934).

## Sequential coverage

- PDF pp. 8–226: reviewed sequentially as mathematical, astronomical, geographical, calendrical and astrolabe foundations. Technical concepts were noted, but non-astrological background material is not promoted merely for completeness.
- PDF pp. 227–248 / §§347–380: reviewed. Astrology proper begins; sign natures, sign classifications, aspects, non-aspect relations, triplicities and seasonal modalities.
- PDF pp. 249–278 / §§381–447: reviewed. Planetary natures, benefic/malefic doctrine, sect, planetary correspondences, years, firdaria, orbs, domiciles, exaltations, triplicity rulers, planetary aspects and friendship/enmity.
- PDF pp. 279–290 / §§448–460: reviewed. Half-signs, faces/figures, Hindu decanates, terms, ninths, twelfths, degree classifications and planetary wells.
- PDF pp. 292–351 / §§461–530: reviewed through the conclusion of the astrology text. House indications, lots, solar conditions, application mechanics, dignity and accidental conditions, divisions of judicial astrology, nativities, elections and questions.
- PDF pp. 352–354: index only; checked, no further source doctrine.

**Sequential review of al-Bīrūnī is complete.**

## Editorial rule applied

Only evidence-grade A or B material is stored as a source interpretation. Long catalogues, repetitions and mere mentions remain in the source corpus but are not promoted to lexicon entries unless they define or substantially clarify a coherent technical concept.

The long tables of individual house topics, lots, planetary correspondences, mundane assignments and similar catalogues were reviewed but not exploded into hundreds of dictionary entries.

## Notable distinctions preserved

- Halb and hayz are distinct; hayz includes the halb condition plus sign-gender conformity.
- Feral passage and void of course are distinct conditions.
- Return, evasion, intervention, abscission of light, refranation and prevention are not collapsed into one generic impediment.
- The combust way is a zodiacal interval, not solar combustion.
- Mubtazz/almuten, hyleg and kadkhuda/alcocoden are separate technical roles.
- Al-Bīrūnī explicitly limits astrology and criticizes attempts to infer arbitrary hidden thoughts or objects beyond the stated question.
