## Editorial rule applied

Only evidence-grade A or B material is promoted into terms or source interpretations. Long delineation catalogues, medical and sexual character lists, electional recipes, weather catalogues, and repeated examples are reviewed but not turned into large numbers of weak encyclopedia entries.

Translator and editor notes are used only for quality control and vocabulary awareness. They are not attributed to Antiochus. This is especially important where Robert Hand or Robert Schmidt propose identifications with later terminology.

## High-value doctrine retained

Part I provides unusually compact technical definitions for Hellenistic sign relationships and configurations. The extraction retains co-rising/equal-ascension sympathy, hearing and seeing, looking ahead, hurling of rays, spear-bearing, position upon the tenth place, affliction, places for and without dealings, counter-revolution, reciprocal mastership, strict contact/application, intervention, surpassing, the Moon’s bond, void course, juxtaposition, containment, chariots, rejoicing, and under-the-beams doctrine.

Existing canonical entries receive Antiochus interpretations where appropriate, especially sect, triplicity rulers, faces/decans, terms/bounds, application, void of course, under the rays, Part of Fortune and planetary years.

The source’s aspect doctrine is deliberately kept source-specific. Antiochus distinguishes zodiacal configuration from relations based on ascensional times and from directional relations such as looking ahead and hurling rays. These should not be silently translated into modern aspect terminology.

The individual lot catalogue and the extensive decan, natal, medical, sexual and electional delineations were reviewed but not promoted wholesale. The project records reusable technical structures rather than every predictive statement.
