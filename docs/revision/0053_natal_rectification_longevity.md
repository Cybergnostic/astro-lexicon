# Revision batch 7 — natal, rectification and longevity terminology

Implemented in `db/seed/0053_cross_source_revision_natal_rectification_longevity.sql`.

## Rectification taxonomy

The revision distinguishes the general technique **Natal Rectification** from three separately defined historical methods in the current corpus:

- **Animodar / Namudar** — prenatal conjunction/opposition + strongest dignity ruler + angular correction. Bonatti, Lilly and al-Bīrūnī independently describe the same basic procedure.
- **Trutine / Scrutiny of Hermes** — Moon-at-birth / conception relationship. Lilly explicitly distinguishes it from Animodar.
- **Rectification by Accidents** — Lilly’s preferred method, testing an estimated birth time against dated events and primary directions.

The methods are linked to Natal Rectification rather than treated as synonyms for the general procedure.

## Hyleg, Kadkhuda and Anareta kept distinct

The three longevity roles are now separated cleanly:

- **Hyleg / Apheta** — the aphetic life-signifying planet or place whose directions are followed.
- **Kadkhuda / Alcochodon** — the dignity ruler related to the Hyleg that supplies the year-giving function.
- **Anareta / Interficient Factor** — the destructive factor in the same longevity framework.

Lilly’s separate provisional **Alcochodon** term is merged into **Kadkhuda**. The variants *Alcocoden*, *Alcochodon*, *Alcochoden* and *Giver of Years* remain aliases.

The Anareta stays quality 4 because the sources disagree over its exact ontological range: Lilly discusses competing planetary candidates, while al-Bīrūnī also allows multiple destructive places/degrees. The definition therefore does not falsely reduce the term to one universal planet.

Detailed mortality recipes remain source material and are not converted into public operating instructions.

## Almudabit pruned as a duplicate mechanism

Bonatti’s **Almudabit / Almudebit** is not retained as a separate canonical ruler. In the relevant passage the planet is selected by the same prevailing-dignity logic and the continuation refers to the same planet as the **al-mubtazz**. The source name is therefore preserved as a specialized alias/use of **Almuten**.

## Lord of the Geniture versus Almuten of the Figure

These are linked but not merged.

Lilly defines **Almuten of the Figure** as the planet strongest in the whole chart by essential and accidental dignity. His preferred **Lord of the Geniture** criterion is extremely close to that rule, but he explicitly records competing older definitions of the Lord of the Geniture and treats it as a natal governing role. The relation is therefore `closely-corresponds-to`, not synonymy.

## Jarbakhtar / Divisor

Bonatti’s **Jarbakhtar / Divisor** remains a separate technical entry for the Ascendant/divisor directed through successive bounds, with bound rulers disposing successive periods. It is linked as a specialized form of primary direction.

The definition remains quality 4 because the wider transmission varies over whether the name denotes the directed Ascendant/divisor itself or the bound-direction procedure.
